05 · Clear space & minimum size
Clear space on all four sides equals one fifth of the medallion diameter (0.2 × ⌀). Nothing — type, rules, image edges — enters that field. For horizontal lockups measure from the outer bounding box of the full lockup.
Minimum sizes
Stacked lockup — print 28 mm wide · screen 110 px wide.
Horizontal lockup — print 38 mm wide · screen 150 px wide.
Emblem alone — print 12 mm · screen 44 px.
Below 44 px on screen or 10 mm in print, switch to the favicon reduction.
Typeface
The original wordmark is a geometric sans in the Futura / Century Gothic lineage — closest widely available match: Jost* (Google Fonts), then Poppins. In these files the type is drawn as geometry, not set, so no licence or font install is needed.

Notes
“reversed” carries a dark proof background; “reversed-clear” is the transparent production file.
No gradients, no shadows, no clipping masks anywhere.
The stepped band, fretwork and letterforms are monoline stroke geometry — run one Expand / Outline Stroke pass before sending to a cutter or embroiderer.
